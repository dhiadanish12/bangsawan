AKMAL HELMY

Akmal Helmy, the Lead Photographer at Bangsawan Pictures, brings a unique blend of technical knowledge and creative vision to his craft. With a background in Mechanical Engineering, he has successfully translated his academic expertise into the world of photography and videography. His ability to understand complex technical setups gives him an edge in ensuring precision and efficiency in every production.
